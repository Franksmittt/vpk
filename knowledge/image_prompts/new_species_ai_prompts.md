   - Leadwood trees (gnarled, ancient bark)
   - Iron stone ridges
   - Dry savanna grasses

2. **Lighting**: Golden hour (4:30-6:00 PM South African time) with warm color temperature (4500K-5500K)

3. **Style**: National Geographic quality, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, Iron Mountain aesthetic

4. **Avoid**: Blood, gore, aggressive poses, overly dark shadows, clichéd safari brochure look

5. **Color Variants**: For Golden/King Wildebeest and Impala variants, emphasize the unique coloration while maintaining biological accuracy
