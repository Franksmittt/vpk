## NOTES FOR AI IMAGE GENERATION

### Key Specifications for All Images:
- **Location Context**: Waterberg region, Thabazimbi, Limpopo, South Africa
- **Environment**: Open grasslands, bushveld, light woodlands, red ochre soil (hematite-rich), Mopane trees, Acacia trees
- **Lighting**: Golden hour (4:30-6:00 PM or early morning), warm directional light (4500K-5500K)
- **Style**: National Geographic style, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, Iron Mountain aesthetic
- **Track Dimensions**: 9-10cm x ~8cm (3.5-4 x 3.1 inches) for mature bull front hooves - accurately proportioned
- **Avoid**: Blood, gore, dead animals, exaggerated proportions, clichéd safari aesthetic

### Golden Wildebeest-Specific Characteristics:
- **Color Pattern**: Coat ranges from flaxen, yellowish-gold in younger animals/cows to deep, burnished copper or bronze in mature bulls
- **Mane and Tail**: Flaxen or light brown, often with distinct white or cream element running through beard (unlike jet-black mane of Blue Wildebeest)
- **Facial Mask**: Critical indicator of maturity - while body remains golden, face of dominant mature bull often darkens significantly, appearing almost slate-grey or black on muzzle and forehead
- **Lateral Striping**: "Brindled" vertical stripes still present but often fainter or appear as darker shade of rust against golden background (rather than black against grey)
- **Build**: Front-heavy silhouette - massive forequarters, sloping back, pronounced shoulder hump
- **Horns**: Lyre-shaped (parentheses shape), extending outwards then curving sharply upwards and inwards, with thickened bosses in mature bulls
- **Size**: Mature bull: 230-290 kg (500-640 lb), 127-152 cm (50-60 in) at shoulder
- **Origin**: Limpopo River basin, Tuli Block region, Botswana. First recorded Golden Wildebeest bull captured by Alec Rough on Swinburne farm, Limpopo Valley (early 1990s)

### Genetic and Economic Context:
- **Genetic Origin**: Autosomal recessive trait. DNA analysis (Dr. Antoinette Kotze) confirmed 100% Connochaetes taurinus - recessive gene analogous to blue eyes in humans
- **Breeding**: Golden x Golden = 100% Golden. Golden x Blue = 100% "Splits". Split x Split = 25% Golden, 50% Split, 25% Blue
- **Economic Value**: Trophy fee ranges $3,000-$4,500 USD (vs $1,100-$1,650 for Blue Wildebeest) - 2.5x-3.0x relative value factor
- **Reputation**: Known as "The Poor Man's Buffalo" - extreme toughness, can absorb poorly placed shots, requires heavy-caliber rifles and premium bullets
