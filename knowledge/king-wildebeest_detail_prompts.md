## NOTES FOR AI IMAGE GENERATION

### Key Specifications for All Images:
- **Location Context**: Waterberg region, Thabazimbi, Limpopo, South Africa
- **Environment**: Open grasslands, bushveld, light woodlands, red ochre soil (hematite-rich), Mopane trees, Acacia trees
- **Lighting**: Golden hour (4:30-6:00 PM or early morning), warm directional light (4500K-5500K)
- **Style**: National Geographic style, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, Iron Mountain aesthetic
- **Track Dimensions**: 100-120mm x 65-85mm (4-4.7 x 2.6-3.3 inches) for mature bull front hooves - accurately proportioned
- **Avoid**: Blood, gore, dead animals, exaggerated proportions, clichéd safari aesthetic

### King Wildebeest-Specific Characteristics:
- **Color Pattern**: Dark coat (dark brown to black), prominent white saddle-like flank marking, white or cream mane and tail, pinkish skin coloration around nasal area and eyes
- **Build**: Front-heavy silhouette - massive forequarters, sloping back, pronounced shoulder hump
- **Horns**: Lyre-shaped (parentheses shape), extending outwards then curving sharply upwards and inwards, with thickened bosses in mature bulls
- **Size**: Mature bull: 250-290 kg (550-640 lb), 150 cm (59 in) at shoulder
- **Behavior**: Gregarious, territorial bulls during rut, grazing succession with zebras, water-dependent
- **Origin**: Tuli Block, Botswana - recessive genetic variant of Blue Wildebeest

### Genetic and Economic Context:
- **Genetic Origin**: Recessive mutation from Tuli Block, Botswana. Not distinct subspecies but color variant
- **Economic Value**: Commands premium trophy fees ($3,000-$10,000 vs $900-$1,200 for Blue Wildebeest)
- **Management**: King x King = 100% King calves. King x Blue = 100% "Split" calves. Split x Split = 25% King, 50% Split, 25% Blue
- **Reputation**: Known as "The Poor Man's Buffalo" - extreme toughness, can absorb poorly placed shots, requires heavy-caliber rifles and premium bullets
